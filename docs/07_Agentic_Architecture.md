# Agentic Architecture and Workflow Controls

Status: Draft v0.1 | 2026-10-14

## Roles
Supervisor; intake; member context; coverage research; damage evidence; provider matching; parts sourcing; scheduling; communications; fraud risk; settlement support.

## Trust boundary
Supervisor may delegate read-only or proposal tools; durable workflow controls state. Humans authorize coverage denial, liability, payment and restricted spending.

## Tool contracts
JSON schema, identity and tenant scope, least privilege, timeout, retries, idempotency and audit provenance for each tool.

## Grounding
Cite policy sources; never fabricate dealer inventory or provider appointments.

## Evaluation
Golden datasets for extraction, tool routing, injection attempts, hallucinations, PII leakage, bias, override rates and task accuracy.

## Failure policy
Low confidence or missing dependency generates a human work item rather than fabricated output.
