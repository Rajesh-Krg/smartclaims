# SmartClaims Business Context

Status: Draft v0.1 | 2026-10-14

## Value
Reduce claim handoff time through authorized AI-assisted coordination. Treat 10x acceleration as an experimental hypothesis, not a guarantee.

## Stakeholders
Insurers, third-party administrators, repair networks, parts suppliers, members, adjusters and compliance staff.

## Capabilities
FNOL; member and policy context; case management; provider network; dealer procurement; settlement support; notifications; acknowledgments; analytics and audit.

## KPIs
Median FNOL-to-triage; claim cycle time p50/p90; manual touches; supplier response time; rework; complaints; SLA breaches.

## Market entry
Pilot repair/parts coordination AI workers before attempting to replace a core insurer platform. Validate buyer, access to policy data, integrations, jurisdiction, staffing and budget.
